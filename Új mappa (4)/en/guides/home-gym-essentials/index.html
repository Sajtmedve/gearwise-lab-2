Home gym essentials: build a workout setup on any budget

Updated: 5 November 2026

You can train your whole body at home with three items: a mat, a set of resistance bands and an adjustable weight. Start there, train for a month, and only then buy more. Most unused home gym equipment was bought too early.

Step 1: Measure your space

  • Floor space: about 2 × 2 m is enough for bodyweight training and dumbbells.
  • Ceiling height: check it before buying anything you lift overhead or a pull-up bar.
  • Flooring: protect wooden floors and reduce noise with a thick mat or rubber tiles, especially in a flat.
  • Storage: adjustable equipment saves a lot of room compared with full dumbbell racks.

Step 3: Upgrades once you train regularly

  • Pull-up bar: one of the best upper-body tools. Doorway models need a sturdy frame; check the maximum load and your door width.
  • Adjustable bench: unlocks presses, rows and step-ups.
  • Kettlebell: great for swings and conditioning; one medium weight is a good start.
  • Jump rope: the cheapest cardio equipment there is.

Step 4: Bigger purchases (only if you know you will use them)

Cardio machines such as an exercise bike, rowing machine or treadmill take space and cost the most. Ask yourself:

  1. Have I trained consistently for at least two months?
  2. Where will it stand, and will noise bother neighbours?
  3. Does it need a subscription to use its main features?
